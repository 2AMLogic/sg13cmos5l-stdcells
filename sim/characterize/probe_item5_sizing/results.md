**Reading.** Baseline reproduces the recorded five SS failures (table below);
widening the output PMOS clears every arc at every ratified corner —
`v4_pmos_1680` (the sizing this probe recommends, +50 % PMOS drive) reaches a
worst margin of **+0.4113 ns** at the failing point, `v1_pmos_2240` (×2) one
of +0.5393 ns at twice the added P-diffusion width. Narrowing the NMOS stack
alone (`v2_nmos_560`) does **not** clear `s5l_nand4_1` `Y<-C`/`Y<-D` — the
rise bottleneck is the parallel-PMOS pull-up fighting the partially-on stack,
not the stack's own width. Outcome per the Curator Enhancement's cases: **(i)
a resized `_1` clears the bound** — no new cell is needed.

- commands, one per run:

```
python3 sim/characterize/probe_item5_sizing/gen_probe.py
klt sim sim/characterize/probe_item5_sizing/gen/<variant>/probe.<corner>.request.json \
    --backend local --pdk-root "$PDK_ROOT" -o <scratch dir>
```

TT / FF numbers are corroborating evidence only (the row binds at SS): the
re-characterization follow-on records the resized cells properly, append-only.
At SS the fall-side arcs move +2.9 to +4.8 ps (added drain capacitance on Y),
except `s5l_nand3b_1_y_a_n_f` at +27.4 ps: the wider output PMOS adds gate load
to the internal `A_N_b` node driven by the cell's own inverter, which that one
arc's path crosses (v0 1.42359 → v4 1.45103 ns; it still passes at +1.0564 ns
of margin); the rise-side arcs improve by ~0.4–0.5 ns.
